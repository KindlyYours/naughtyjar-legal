SUPPORT

How can we help?

Straightforward answers, without exposing what is yours.

Where can I see a person’s JAR and closed cycles?

In Accomplices, tap the archive button for a person to open their JAR profile. It shows the current JAR, its value, and closed cycles with thumbnails. There is currently one JAR per accomplice; multiple JARs and occasions remain on the roadmap.

Getting started

What is NaughtyJar?

It is a private app for recording moments, tracking promises, and closing cycles with an accomplice. The free version covers the core purpose: one accomplice, unlimited moments and promises, with no time limit.

How do I change currency?

Choose the starting currency in Settings. You can also change it in each accomplice’s details while a cycle has no records. Existing cycles keep their original currency to preserve the history.

Can I use the app without permissions?

Yes. Photos, notifications, biometrics, and widgets are optional. The app works without them.

Moments, promises, and cycles

What happens when I close a cycle?

If there are pending promises, you can carry them into the next cycle, mark them unfulfilled, or cancel and stay in the current cycle. Nothing is deleted or moved without your choice.

What happens when a promise is fulfilled?

You can choose to add it as a Moment using the amount set for the cycle. This increases the total and adds a purple charm. Deleting the promise also removes its linked Moment.

Does an overdue promise disappear?

No. It remains pending until you fulfil it, mark it unfulfilled, edit it, or delete it.

Can I recover a deleted promise or Moment?

No. Deletion is permanent, so please confirm carefully before proceeding.

Privacy and security

Where is my data stored?

In the current version, records stay on your device. There is no NaughtyJar account, developer server, or cross-device sync.

How do I protect the app?

In Settings, enable NaughtyJar’s lock and use a separate passcode and/or biometrics when supported. Biometrics only appears after the lock is enabled.

I forgot the app code. What happens when I recover access?

Device authentication starts recovery, but does not immediately open your data. You must create a new NaughtyJar code before continuing. The recovery date, time, and available location are stored in Settings → Security. When you set up the app code, you can choose whether to authorize location for this purpose; if authorized, it is collected only during a recovery. Recovery continues if location is unavailable. After you save the new code, if notifications are authorized, you receive a discreet alert to review a security update. The delay starts at two hours and can be changed in Settings → Security. Three consecutive incorrect passcodes also temporarily lock access and are recorded; normal unlocks are not.

Can the widget reveal private information?

The widget is optional and Premium. It is technically implemented, but purchases are not active yet. When Premium access is available, you can hide its amount and show a name, initials only, or no identity. Also check iOS sensitive-content options for the Lock Screen.

How do I delete everything?

In Settings, choose “Delete all data” and confirm with biometrics or your NaughtyJar passcode. It permanently removes accomplices, photos, moments, promises, cycles, preferences, widget content, and the app passcode.

Backup and Premium

Can I buy Premium now?

No. Purchases are not active for launch yet. Some Premium areas exist for preparation and testing, but are not a commercial promise. See the roadmap for the current product status.

How will backup work?

Premium backup is technically implemented, but purchases are not active yet. When access is available, you will be able to create a password-protected encrypted .njbackup file and save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or another installed provider. It includes security activity, including available recovery locations, but never the app passcode or biometric settings. Restore first shows a summary and replaces current data only after confirmation.

What if I forget the backup password?

It cannot be recovered. NaughtyJar never stores or receives this password; without it, the encrypted file cannot be restored.

Is there iPhone and iPad sync?

Not yet. Future sync will only be considered after clear rules for security, conflicts, deletion, and recovery are in place.
