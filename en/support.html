Getting started

What is NaughtyJar?

It is a private Christmas Jar: you keep intimate moments, the Jar grows, and you choose a special gift.

Can I use the app without permissions?

Yes. Photos, notifications, biometrics, and the widget are optional.

Jar and moments

How does the Christmas Jar work?

Record moments throughout the year and follow the saved value. The cycle is kept in the archive after Christmas.

Backup and privacy

Where is my data?

On your device. There is no NaughtyJar account or active sync.

How do backups work?

They are free and encrypted with a password you choose. The password cannot be recovered.
