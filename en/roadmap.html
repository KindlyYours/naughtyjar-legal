ROADMAP

First, do it well.

NaughtyJar grows carefully: privacy and trust come before new features.

Status reviewed August 27, 2026.

Available in the free version

  • One accomplice, unlimited moments, and unlimited promises.
  • Active cycle, optional notes, and reminders.
  • Per-cycle currency, iOS language, and an optional encrypted photo.
  • Passcode and/or biometric lock and complete data deletion.

Premium: implemented, but not available yet

Purchases are not active and there is no final commercial decision. However, these features are already implemented and are currently undergoing internal validation:

  • Multiple accomplices.
  • Complete history and closed-cycle archive.
  • Statistics and cycle comparison.
  • Widgets with privacy controls.
  • Complete encrypted, restorable manual backups in .njbackup files.

If a paid option exists later, it will be for genuinely optional extras — never to remove the free version’s core purpose, privacy, security, or data deletion.

Being considered after the free release

  • A readable export for consultation outside the app, separate from backup.
  • Cycle goals, multiple jars/occasions, favourites, summaries, and customisation.
  • Cross-device sync, only after security, conflict, deletion, and recovery are fully defined.

What this is not

Roadmap ideas are possibilities, not dates or guarantees. Direction can change with testing and real feedback. The rule remains: fix and validate first; distribute next; only then add extras.
